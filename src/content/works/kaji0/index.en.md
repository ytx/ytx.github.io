---
title: KAJI⭕️
summary: Record and share household chores.
---

A web app for families and small groups to record and share household chores. Register your chores, then log one with a single tap when it is done. Records appear on a timeline and each chore earns points, so everyone can see who did how much. It is designed for smartphone browsers and can be added to the home screen.

Chores you want help with can be posted as a request to a specific person or as an open call to anyone, with bonus points attached. There are also sprints, where everyone tackles a set of chores within a time window; house rules, a versioned document that members acknowledge; and notifications to Discord and LINE.

A tablet in the living room can run Center Mode. It shows today's chores as tiles, and anyone can log a chore without signing in by tapping "done by ...". With a stats panel, a clock and a photo screensaver, it works as an always-on display.
