---
title: l_timer
summary: A lecture timer with lecture/break cycles, an exercise timer and notes.
---

A timer for running lectures and training sessions. It alternates lecture (50 min by default) and break (10 or 60 min) periods and marks each boundary with a warning chime a few minutes before the end, an end bell, and a periodic tone while running over. The end time can be nudged by 1 or 10 minutes with the ± buttons.

During a lecture you can run an independent exercise timer on top. Give it a title, queue the next exercise in advance, and it switches over automatically when the current one ends. While an exercise runs the lecture clock shrinks, and the exercise pauses when a break starts.

On the right is a small editor for lecture notes: headings, bullet lists and bold only, with several documents you can save and switch between. It is meant to stay open in a browser on the PC in front of you.
