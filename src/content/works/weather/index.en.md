title: weather
summary: A one-screen, always-on forecast for a single location.
---

An always-on forecast for one location, laid out to fit a single landscape screen. Current weather, temperature, humidity, wind and waves sit on the left; hourly forecasts for today and tomorrow with a temperature and precipitation chart are at the top right; the weekly forecast is at the bottom right. Nothing needs scrolling.

The data combines the Japan Meteorological Agency forecasts (regional and weekly) with Open-Meteo hourly data, and the location is chosen by searching a place name. It refreshes every 10 minutes and keeps the last result on screen if a fetch fails.

For a dedicated display you can pass the location and theme in the URL and bookmark it, so the page starts showing the forecast as soon as it opens. It is meant to live on a monitor or a tablet in landscape.
