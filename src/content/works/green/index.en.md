---
title: green (happy green tiles)
summary: Tiles for to-dos, logs and timers.
---

Everything you mean to do today becomes a tile on one dashboard. The colour of a tile is its state: red for not started, yellow for in progress, blue while you are waiting on someone else, green once it is done. If every tile is green by the end of the day, it was a good day.

Tiles come from six sources: to-dos, routines that repeat on chosen weekdays, intervals that turn red once enough days have passed since you last did them, checklists you tick off, timers, and one-tap records. Starting the day's plan pulls in that day's routines and any intervals that have come due; anything else you add from the to-do list. Tap targets are large, so it works as it is on a tablet.

Records you tap and tiles you finish stay in the life log, and a work report (Markdown) can be assembled from them per work area, split into what was done and what comes next, with any line you would rather leave out unchecked. It is a PWA, so it works offline on its own; sign in and it syncs across devices.
