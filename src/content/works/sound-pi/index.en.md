---
title: sound-pi
summary: One computer's audio, played on up to four headphones or speakers at once.
---

An audio splitter built from a Raspberry Pi 4 and a small 3.5-inch touch screen. It connects to a PC or Mac with a single USB-C cable and appears to the computer as a USB audio device. The sound it receives is played on up to four USB or Bluetooth headphones and speakers at the same time.

Volume and mute are set separately for each output, and turning the knob (a rotary encoder) on the unit changes the volume of the selected output. The screen shows VU meters or a spectrum, switched by touch. Bluetooth and Wi-Fi are also set up from the unit's own screen.

Play/pause and track buttons on the headphones are passed to the computer as media keys, and their volume buttons change that output's volume. The source is not public; please get in touch if you are interested.
